Since its founding in 1968 in Ahmedabad, La Femme has defined luxury bridal beauty and salon craftsmanship. Over five decades of passion and continuous refinement have shaped an indelible legacy of timeless beauty, haute hair styling, and couture makeup artistry.

OUR HERITAGE & VISIONARIES

THE LA FEMME LEGACY

Founded in 1968, La Femme represents a pioneering milestone in India’s luxury beauty industry.

MR. SATISH CHRISTIAN

FOUNDER & VISIONARY

Pioneered the founding of La Femme in 1968 in Ahmedabad. With visionary foresight and dedication, he laid the groundwork for over five decades of luxury salon culture and beauty excellence.

AANALL CHRISTIAN

HAUTE STYLIST & CREATIVE DIRECTOR

Beginning her stellar career in 1997, Aanall Christian elevated La Femme into a nationally recognized haute bridal and avantgarde powerhouse, winning prestigious national awards and setting new standards in beauty artistry.
